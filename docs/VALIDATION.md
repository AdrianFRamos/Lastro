# Market validation

Technology is not the same as validation.

Before submission: at least five structured conversations:
- 2 producers/operators;
- 1 traceability/certification participant;
- 1 slaughterhouse/export/compliance participant;
- 1 agricultural risk/insurance/finance participant.

Record the current process, concrete failure, frequency, cost/risk when known, systems used, accepted evidence, objections, buyer, and real pilot interest.

“I thought it was cool” does not count.

The objective is to confirm or falsify hypotheses; never force the outcome.
